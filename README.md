# IDREMIS (Integrated Disaster Response and Emergency Management Information System)

**City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO)**  
General Santos City, South Cotabato

---

## 📌 About the Project

**IDREMIS** is a centralized web-based disaster response and management information system designed to streamline emergency operations, dispatch responders, monitor evacuation center capacities, and coordinate relief supplies in General Santos City.

---

## 🚀 System Features

* **Public Emergency Dispatch:** Direct community access to submit real-time rescue and incident requests.
* **Administrative Authentication:** Secure portal access for CDRRMO officers, barangay officials, rescue units, and medics.
* **Incident & Response Tracking:** Real-time logging and status updates for disaster incidents across all barangays.
* **Evacuation Center Monitoring:** Live tracking of center capacities, medical staff assignments, and occupancy rates.
* **Relief Supplies Inventory:** Centralized management of food packs, medical kits, and non-food resources.

---

## 📊 System Modules & Tables (Task 4 Update)

Integrated static management tables pre-populated with operational data:
1. **Emergency Incidents & Response Log** - Tracks incident IDs, affected barangays, severity levels, and assigned teams.
2. **Evacuation Center Occupancy Monitoring** - Tracks center capacities, current occupants, and availability.
3. **Relief Supplies & Inventory Management** - Monitors stock levels, categories, and distribution targets.
